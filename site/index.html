barça signings ledger · 2010–2026

By Arnaud Azenui

Every player Barça paid a transfer fee for, judged on what he did at the club (against players in the same role, with credit for trophies he helped win) and on the money that came back when he left.

Built by Arnaud Azenui · github.com/ArnaudAzenuiData: transfermarkt-datasets (to July 2026), Capology payrolls, hand-entered fees for early deals. Made with Python.
